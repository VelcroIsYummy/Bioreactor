# Bioreactor
This project is a expandable and usable bioreactor designed to allow **you** to grow bacteria, fungi, anything you can grow in an expensive bioreactor! Essencially this is a sterile volume, with ports or holes for instumentation (thermocouples, pH sensors, ect.) with software for controlling and managing the systems within.

## Why
Decentralized science is one of the most pivotal steps toward our future, wether it's finding a cure for an obscure disease, or providing resources to those who need it, decentralized science is the solution. Our project enables this, helping fuel a better future for us all. Perhaps one day someone will use it for something like producing insulin in areas that have limited outside access.
